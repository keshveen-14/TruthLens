# Pricing

The Helio H10 costs 6,40,000 rupees and the Helio H15 costs 8,90,000 rupees, before installation. Installation typically costs between 45,000 and 70,000 rupees.

Partner banks offer 0 percent EMI over 12 months on both models.

# Support

Phone support is available from 8 am to 8 pm IST, Monday to Saturday. Email replies arrive within 2 business days. Support staff can view remote diagnostics only with the owner's consent given through the app.

# Returns

Helio accepts returns within 30 days of purchase for units that are unopened and not installed. A restocking fee of 10 percent applies to returns.

# Time-of-use scheduling

The app can schedule charging for off-peak hours. Export of electricity to the grid is possible only where the local utility allows it, and the app has a setting to limit the export.
