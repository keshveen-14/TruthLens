# Safety systems

A battery management system monitors the voltage and temperature of every cell. The unit shuts down automatically if any cell temperature rises above 60 °C.

The cells sit inside a fire-rated steel enclosure. The battery design has passed third-party abuse testing for overcharge, short circuit and crush.

# Warnings

The battery must not be used as the only power source for medical life-support equipment. Install a smoke detector within 3 m of the unit.

If the unit shows signs of fire or swelling, leave the area, keep a distance of at least 10 m and call the emergency services. Do not open the enclosure.

# Backup mode

During a power cut, backup mode supplies an essential-loads circuit only, not the whole home. The H10 can supply at most 5 kW in backup mode and the H15 at most 7.5 kW.
