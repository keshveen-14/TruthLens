# Standard warranty

The standard warranty lasts 10 years. It guarantees that the battery keeps at least 70 percent of its original capacity at the end of the warranty period or after 6,000 cycles, whichever comes first.

The warranty starts on the commissioning date recorded by an authorised installer, not on the purchase date. If more than 6 months pass between purchase and commissioning, the warranty starts 6 months after purchase.

# Extended warranty

An extended warranty that lengthens coverage to 15 years is available. It costs 4 percent of the purchase price and must be bought within 12 months of commissioning. No warranty longer than 15 years is offered.

# Exclusions and transfer

The warranty does not cover damage caused by installation by a person who is not an authorised installer, flooding, ambient temperatures above 50 °C, or any modification of the unit.

The warranty can be transferred once to a new homeowner if the transfer is registered within 60 days of the change of ownership.

# Claims

Replacement units ship within 10 business days of claim approval. On-site labour for a replacement is covered for the first 3 years only.
