# Technical specifications

The Helio H10 has 10 kWh of usable capacity, a continuous power output of 5 kW and a peak output of 7 kW for 10 seconds. It weighs 98 kg and measures 1,050 mm by 600 mm by 190 mm.

The Helio H15 has 15 kWh of usable capacity, a continuous power output of 7.5 kW and a peak output of 10 kW for 10 seconds. It weighs 138 kg and measures 1,350 mm by 600 mm by 190 mm.

# Common specifications

Both models have a round-trip efficiency of 95 percent. The rated cycle life is 6,000 cycles to 70 percent of original capacity. The battery can discharge at temperatures from -10 °C to 50 °C and can charge at temperatures from 0 °C to 45 °C.

The enclosure is rated IP55, which allows outdoor installation in a location covered from direct rain. Noise is below 30 dB at one metre. Backup switchover takes less than 20 milliseconds.

# Connectivity

The battery connects through Wi-Fi at 2.4 GHz or through Ethernet. It has no cellular modem.
