# Helio Home Battery overview

The Helio Home Battery is a wall-mounted energy storage unit for homes. It uses lithium iron phosphate (LFP) cells. It is sold in two models: the Helio H10 with 10 kWh of usable capacity and the Helio H15 with 15 kWh of usable capacity.

Both models work with rooftop solar systems and can also charge from the grid during off-peak hours. Up to 4 units can be connected together on one site to increase capacity.

# App and updates

The battery is controlled through the Helio app, which is available for iOS and Android. The app shows charge level, solar production and household consumption in real time. Firmware updates are delivered over Wi-Fi and are installed automatically at night unless the owner turns automatic updates off.

# Company

Helio assembles its batteries at a single factory in Pune, India. Helio sells the battery in India only at present.
