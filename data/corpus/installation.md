# Installation requirements

Installation must be carried out by a Helio-certified installer. The unit must be mounted on a masonry or concrete wall, or on a timber stud wall fitted with a backing board.

The bottom of the unit must be at least 300 mm above the floor. Leave 500 mm of clearance at each side and 200 mm of clearance above the unit for ventilation.

The battery must not be installed in bedrooms, bathrooms or other habitable rooms. A garage, utility room or covered outdoor wall are suitable locations.

# Electrical and timing

The unit needs a dedicated 32 A circuit breaker. A typical installation of an H10 takes one day with two installers.

The installer submits the grid connection application to the local utility. Approval typically takes 2 to 6 weeks.

# Commissioning

The installer commissions the battery through the Helio app by scanning the QR code on the unit. Commissioning records the date on which the warranty starts.
